Changelog

0.9

November 2026 · latest
  • Comes back on its own — a dropped connection retries automatically with patient backoff and restores your session, scrollback and SFTP pane included.
  • Snippets — save the commands you type over and over. Paste or Run from the toolbar popover, manage them in the sidebar, share via JSON — and they sync with your other Macs. A few examples already included to get you going.

0.8

November 2026
  • Tags & notes — tag your sessions and spot them at a glance: tag chips are shown under each host in the sidebar, and search works for tags too.
  • Now on Homebrew — brew install --cask knstntnkek/macitty/macitty.

0.7

November 2026
  • File-transfer activity log — a collapsible history pane at the bottom of the Files tab now lists every copy, move, delete, rename and edit with its result and time, so you always know what ran and what failed.
  • Better file browsing — .. navigation rows, editable path fields, hidden-file toggles, right-click menus, and symlink badges on both panes.
  • Tidier Preferences — settings categories now collapse into cards (Option-click to expand or collapse all).

0.6

November 2026
  • Server monitoring — opt in per server and watch live CPU, memory, disk and load right in the session's sidebar row.
  • Monitoring your way — pick a poll interval from 15 seconds to 5 minutes; the first numbers arrive almost instantly.
  • Unlock once — saved secrets now ask for Touch ID or your password a single time per app run; terminals, SFTP and monitors all fill silently after that, and it re-locks with app lock.

0.5

November 2026
  • iCloud sync — optionally keep sessions and settings in step across your Macs through your private iCloud. Credentials are never synced.
  • Automatic updates — MaciTTY checks for new builds itself and installs them on your confirmation.
  • Make it yours — four app icons to choose from, and a language selector right in Preferences.
  • Faster and tidier — saved sessions switch instantly, and Preferences moved to a cleaner two-column layout.
  • SFTP, rounded out — toolbar actions on both panes, recursive remote delete, moving files on the remote, and safeguards against accidental overwrites.
  • Forget me — one click removes every saved password and passphrase for a connection.

0.1

November 2026 · first public release
  • Session library — saved sessions in folders, Default Settings, quick connect and search, with live connections embedded in one window.
  • Every protocol — SSH, Telnet, Rlogin, Raw and Serial.
  • Full authentication — passwords, keyboard-interactive, PPK and OpenSSH keys, agent and GSSAPI, with host-key verification.
  • Tunnels & proxies — local, remote and dynamic forwarding, X11, connection sharing, HTTP/SOCKS proxies and SSH jump hosts.
  • SFTP file browser — dual-pane transfers with drag & drop, and remote files editable in your local editor.
  • Private by design — Keychain + Touch ID secrets, app lock, no telemetry, everything stored locally.
  • Deep customization — colours, cursor styles, keyboard modes, legacy charsets, light/dark/system themes.
  • Power tools — ~/.ssh/config import, session logging, command-line launch, English/German/Russian/Spanish.
