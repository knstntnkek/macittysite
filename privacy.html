Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. The short version: the app collects nothing, and this website counts visits with a cookieless, no-tracking analytics tool.

The MaciTTY app

MaciTTY has no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts — and no servers of ours, so there is nothing to breach and nothing to sell. The app makes network connections only to the servers you connect to.

  • Sessions, folders, tags, notes, snippets and settings live in the app's local storage on your Mac.
  • Passwords and passphrases live in the macOS Keychain — never in session files.
  • Optional iCloud sync uses your own private iCloud (CloudKit); credentials are deliberately never synced, and the feature is off unless you turn it on.
  • Update checks fetch a small feed from this site — the only request the app makes to us. You can turn them off in Settings.

The Privacy & security page in the guide covers the app's side in more detail.

This website

  • Hosting — macitty.app is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address and request details to deliver pages; GitHub's privacy statement covers their side.
  • Analytics — we use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics service. It records that a page was viewed along with the referrer, browser, operating system and country — no IP address is stored, no cookies are set, and nothing follows you across sites. Clicks on the download buttons are counted the same way.
  • No ads, no trackers, no cookies — which is why there's no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the update lands on this page — that is the notification. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
