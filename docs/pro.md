# MaciTTY Pro

MaciTTY is free for everyday use — saved sessions, SSH/SFTP, serial,
snippets, monitoring, remote editing. Pro lifts the library caps and
unlocks the extras — subscribe or buy lifetime in the App Store version;
a lifetime license for the direct download is coming soon.

## What Pro unlocks

- **Unlimited saved sessions** — free keeps 12 usable; extras stay
  saved, locked, never deleted
- **Unlimited connections** — free runs 4 at once
- **Split view** — two terminals side by side in one window
- **iCloud sync** — sessions, default settings and snippets across
  your Macs
- **Session logging** — write terminal output to disk
- **Snippet export** — share snippet libraries as files

Plans: monthly, yearly or lifetime on the App Store. A one-time
lifetime license key for the direct download is **coming soon**.

## Two versions, two purchase channels

MaciTTY ships two builds: the **direct download** from this site and
the **Mac App Store** version (sandboxed — a few power features like
local-shell sessions and agent forwarding stay exclusive to the
direct download).

A Pro purchase covers one channel: App Store purchases (subscription
or lifetime) unlock the App Store version; license keys for the
direct download are coming soon and will cover that build. That's an
Apple rule — App Store apps can't unlock features bought elsewhere.

Your data isn't locked in either way:

- If iCloud sync was on in the version you're leaving, open
  **Settings → iCloud Sync → Restore from iCloud** in the new install —
  a one-time pull that doesn't require Pro.
- Saved passwords live in your Mac's Keychain, which both versions
  share.

## Bought the wrong one? Moving for good?

**Just purchased and it's minutes old:** refund it — App Store refunds
go through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) —
then buy the right one. Fastest path.

**Switching with an existing purchase:** forward your receipt to
support@macitty.app and we'll set you up on the other side — once per
customer. Redemptions on the App Store side are limited by Apple, so
this stays a manual, by-email thing.
